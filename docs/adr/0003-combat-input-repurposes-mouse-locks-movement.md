# Combat verbs repurpose the mouse/pen and lock movement; no gamepad

While Attack or Block is held, the mouse/pen drives the blade's Target Point instead of the camera (with a small rate-capped auto-facing assist so the player isn't left unable to track a circling opponent), and character movement is fully locked for the entire hold — matching how a real fencer plants their feet the moment they commit to a cut or a guard, rather than continuing to strafe mid-swing.

This is mouse/pen-only by design: an analog stick can't trace an arbitrary path, only a direction and magnitude, so gamepad parity was explicitly rejected rather than deferred — supporting it would mean either a fundamentally simpler mechanic on stick or a second, lesser control scheme to maintain.
